ISO 27001

ISO 27001 Implementation, Internal Audit & ISMS Documentation

I help organizations, including SaaS, technology and growing teams, implement, maintain and prepare their Information Security Management System (ISMS) for ISO/IEC 27001 certification.

My experience covers the ISMS lifecycle, from gap assessment, risk assessment and treatment, Statement of Applicability (SoA), policies and procedures and evidence review through internal audit, management review, remediation and certification readiness. I have supported organizations through Stage 1, Stage 2, certification, surveillance and recertification activities.

For technical controls, I work with the responsible technical teams to review evidence, map it to ISO 27001 requirements, identify gaps and track remediation. Hands-on technical configuration and penetration testing/VAPT are outside my scope.

What You Get

  • ISMS Scope & Gap AssessmentISO 27001:2022 gap assessment, prioritized findings and remediation tracker.
  • Risk Assessment & TreatmentRisk methodology, Risk Register, Risk Treatment Plan and SoA.
  • ISMS DocumentationPolicies, procedures, standards, registers, forms, templates and required records.
  • Control & Evidence ReviewEvidence mapped against Clauses 4–10 and applicable Annex A controls.
  • Internal AuditRisk-based audit program, checklist, sampling, report, NCs/OFIs and corrective actions.
  • Management ReviewAgenda, pack, required inputs and outputs, actions and records.
  • Certification ReadinessStage 1, Stage 2, surveillance and recertification support.
  • Cross-Framework MappingISO 27001 mapped to PCI DSS, SOC 2 and data privacy.
